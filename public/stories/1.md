# Part 1

She stood motionless. Frost laid quietly
over the stone steps to the altar.

The altar that was once defiled by the
orcs for demonic purposes. The same
altar that now exuded chill of grave,
humming with residual magic.

Untainted, pure and arcane.

The skulls and the overall decor of
the place were lacking a bit, but it
still looked like a fel shrine.

Crunch. The snow was ever fresh and
another step pulled her towards the
altar.

Silence raised its volume to explosive
levels. The air was static with the
sound of deafness as the storm
had already passed.

You could cut the atmosphere with a
very sharp sword, but it would be
pointless. It would have to be
not only metaphorical, but
also metaphysical.

The snow crunched. Another step
pulled her ever towards the altar.

Upon the altar laid the device. It was
oddly shaped, somewhat elegant, and
quite well crafted. She couldn't quite see
how one would hold it in their hand.

Let alone what its purpose had. There was
that midnight black trail of wire that cut
the white of the snow, and veered off
somewhere else and back again.
And another object.

"Craftfuly affixed to the power amplifier",
Ellie thought, approaching the intruder.
Her artwork was getting handled by some
other's hands but distance couldn't be
cut in time.

The elf warrior stood still once more,
evaluating the instruments attachment
to the apparatus on the altar...

# Part 2

The next few events happened a little
too fast to recall, but I know for a fact
that the goblin woman was not as
comfortable sitting on the ground, as
the elf was with her combat skills.

Ellie could not sense the elf's soul,
until the exact moment that a hard
surface was touching her back.

"That's... mine," she stammered.

"What business do you have in
these frigid parts, goblin?"

"I'm... I'm into acoustics," she panicked.

"Acoustics?"

"Yeah! When the sound goes really,
really far and really loud."

Did this bother Aellia? The goblin
was completely hapless, prone and
vulnerable, yet brimming with some
sort of authority and menace after
that little fist-clenching gesture
with her right arm.

The pause stretched longer than it
was necessary. The night elf shape
vanished from dazed view of little
goblin, who was still plaid on the
once-fluffy layer, which was kinda
getting more cozy by the minute.

She watched the goblin eventually
pull herself together, get up, sigh
quietly, and waddle over to pick
up the instrument.

"Look, I would give a demonstration,
but I can't see a single possibility
of you appreciating the nuances of a
well tuned guitar," the yet unharmed
assaulter projected with almost over
the top, vocally straining tone...

A light pop broke the silence, and
the child-sized creature flexed her
back. She spat a small drop of blood
onto the ground.

"...given your hostile actions towards
one of the best audio engineers this
side of Kalimdor has ever seen!", she
finished with a lot more life behind
her words. No hostility. Some spite.

Aellia, our night elf scout, who had
travelled half a continent had never
before seen such a display of angst.

"So this acoustics, its loud and has
something to do with this device on
top of the altar?"

"Everything is to do with the volume
of air that you can move with a big
enough dynamic!" the goblin's eyes
grew vibrant with excitement, "you
have to understand, the louder the
sound - the further it travels, and,
and when it..."

The goblin coughed up another spurt
of blood on the ground. It stood out
quite brightly, crimson on a white
background. Her back hunched again.

"So, when it bounces off the terrain
around the venue, it can produce all
sorts of standing harmonic waves!"

"Are you hurt?" the night elf ready to
produce a medicinal kit had moved
sideways. Blurry. It wasn't even dark
yet, but she was blending in with the
shadows.

"Yeah, I will live," the artist leaned
onto the stone surface, but her elbow
cut into the edge of the granite a
tiny bit, and the nervous signal
reached the powerful goblin mind at
the speed of sound, with the ensuing
flail visibly upsetting the elf warrior.

# Part 3

Dizzy. This will not work. Concentration
would be required in response to any
further re-actions from this elf.

Who suddenly was not a priority. A
little gremlin hand was tugging at
her cloak, its grin visible before
she could even move her focus in
its direction.

"qaN ehbl, garzlnc ffvZ?" the demon's
voice pried into her mind. Stepping
past any previous defense, it took
over and picked a nibble out of her
soul - "ure'f."

A slender shadow slid past her bent
form, not giving enough time to shove
the imp away.

"I'm not here on demonic duties!"

"Explains the stench of your dress."

Before she could take any closer step,
her arms instinctively refreshed the
spell, granting her sudden sensation
of long missed defense.

The demonic presence lingered casting
no shade over the musical device, it had
already faced the elf from atop of the altar.

A dagger shimmed its way into the stone,
right next to where the imp was standing
not even a moment ago. It took the sign
as clear aggression, and flame began to
gather in its palms.

"I am not going to die here!", she howled,
"I have an interview next week in Gadgetzan!"

The elf resisted.

Ready for the incoming gust of flame coming
from top of the altar, the elf had shifted
her gaze towards the creature. It packed a
vile aroma despite its size.

"The reports were accurate."

The imp's friend was out of range, still readying
a spell. A dark sensation took Aellia by surprise,
with a feeling of incoming weakness
about to take grasp of her chest.

"You've cast the wrong..!" before the imp
could finish the sentence, the elf had
cleaved it back into the Twisting Nether.

# Part 4

Step, followed by another step. Another
step, and distancing reverberations.

The goblin had abandoned all dignity and
was running away at burning pace. Her feet
stung with each step.

The elf warrior surveyed the combat site.
No further hostility. Rage was beginning
to subside, her focus ever present yet
less obstructed by the combat.

The pain never swelled. It fizzled.

The goblin device on top of the altar was
glowing faintly, absorbing the essence
of the slain imp. Aellia approached it carefully.

Without its master in proximity, fumbled
patchwork of components laid evident
on the stone surface.

The elf took a swing, sending the goblin
amplifier device vanishing down the
mountain side.

# Part 5

The purring of the beast kept comfort
to the elf. The encounter with the goblin
warlock could have turned her soul into
a stone, had she had the luck play out
differently at the combat site.

Report was filed and passed on to Kathora
of the events at combat site. The travel to
scout the Far Watch would have taken nights.

Kathora, the younger elf had taken ranks
very quickly. She had passed training
first of the new recruits with stunning credentials.

Aellia had finished her meal, and with a
few nimble moves stoked the campfire
and looked towards the goblin town.

Everlook, a disarmingly charming town.
With the wildlife surrounding it ready
to disembowel you with any misstep.

The gryphon rider was camped out all day
by an orcish rogue. The Horde general
posted in this town was hostile ever since
her first arrival. Her alertness had never lowered.

He posed no threat still, as Aellia was wary
of showing aggression in front of the
guards. The goblin town did not appear
as such, but could turn into a hostile
and very weaponized fortress with any
misstep.

The hum of endless productivity kept her
from resting. Carving out the wildlife
on return to camp took toll, her food
supply was dwindling and Kathora had
requested the medicinal kit for her
return to Darnassus.

# Part 6

The moon's light shimmer was loudly
interrupted by the singing smell of
demonic magic.

It subsided as fast as it had erupted,
leaving an amorphous volume of smoke
where the impact took shape. It rippled.

The nimble elf kept her shield readied to
deflect the incoming volley. Yet it missed
again,  and left the rock below her feet
humming a dark and menacing melody.

The crickety pair of ambushers found their
path of escape blocked off.

Aellia's tactical mastery had paid off.

"Might we make introduction, then?"

"You've destroyed my life's possessions,
you monster!" yelled the goblin shape.
A different spell had fired off her finger-
tips, surprising Aellia with its eagerness.

She froze in place. It connected, pulsed
and resonated in her chest, then found
the ground as it left through her feet.

"I had put every last piece of silver into
that power amplifier, and you couldn't
even have the generosity to gather it
to report to your superiors!? Throw
it down into the canyon!? Really?!"

The goblin's stance shifted as she aimed
another spell at Aellia. A rare sensation
of defenselessness took over. She could
not force a battle cry, or take a step.

The imp creature caught her eye. It was
airheaded and impatiently muttering
to its master, whilst not participating in
the unfolding combat.

As the torrent of foul harm was heading
her way, Aellia charged towards the
pair of casters. In a few moments,
she sensed that they would be ready
to unleash their ultimate attack, but
that never unfolded.

Her shield, as ordinary as it was, did not
respect the will of the enemy warlock.
It did not glow like the stone in the
ground had done.

Instead, Aellia jammed the vile blast
directly into the goblin's puny torso.
Ellie's eyes lit up for a second, but the
continued follow-through of the strike
diffused kinetic force into the robed
mage, out of its path, and the materia
bowed.

A small clap of green tonality could be seen
from the inn below the hill the battle was
happening on.
